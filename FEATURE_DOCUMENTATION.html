MOD-01: Authentication & Dynamic RBAC Security Layer

1.1 User Authentication & Password Change /login, /change-password
Enforces encrypted session credentials (zazu_logged_in). Automatic logout upon inactivity, brute-force protection, and secure password updates with current credential verification.
1.2 Dynamic Navigation Menu Manager /menu-management, /menu-management/reorder
Construct and reorder menu trees dynamically. Allows admins to configure parent groups, child menu routes, and FontAwesome icons without modifying code files.
1.3 Role-Based Permission Matrix /role-management, /role-permission
Assign granular permissions per menu per role: View (read-only), Add (create transaction), Edit (modify records), and Delete (soft-delete).

MOD-02: Master Data Management Configuration Engine

2.1 Company Profiles & Multi-Bank Accounts /company-list, /company-bank-list
Maintains corporate identity, CR and VAT numbers, invoice headers, and multi-currency bank accounts with IBAN, Swift, and automated general ledger links.
2.2 Customer & Vendor Directories /customer-list, /vendor-list
Customer & supplier records with CR/VAT numbers, GPS delivery coordinates, multiple contact persons, and opening ledger balance initializations.
2.3 Items, Categories, Brands & Units (UOM) /items-list, /category-list, /uom-list
Central catalog with item descriptions, HSN codes, default VAT rates, and specification sheet file attachments.

MOD-03: Customer Tender & Sales Execution Customer Side

3.1 Tender Enquiry Registration (RFQ) /add-tender-enquiry, /tender-enquiry-list
Log customer requests, deadline dates, technical specifications PDF upload, and item line registrations. Automatically generates an Enquiry ID.
3.2 Tender Quotation & Pricing Engine /tender-quotation-add, /tender-quotation-print/(:num)
Auto-fetches enquiry items. Supports multi-currency conversion, unit costs, profit markups, and VAT calculation:
Line Net Rate = Unit Cost × (1 + Margin %)
Gross Total = Total Subtotal + Total VAT + Freight / Handling
Includes one-click branded quotation PDF export.
3.3 Customer PO Award Tracking /customer-tender-po-add
Record client contract awards, input customer PO reference numbers, upload signed PO copies, and confirm ordered line quantities.
3.4 Delivery Challan (DC) Dispatch Note /tender-dc-add, /tender-dc-print/(:num)
Issue dispatch notes with vehicle numbers, driver contact, dispatch quantities, and legal gate-pass verification sheets.
3.5 Commercial Tax Invoice Generation /tender-invoice-add, /tender-po-invoice-print/(:num)
Auto-generate commercial tax invoices linked to PO and DC shipments with automated VAT breakdowns and payment bank selection.

MOD-04: Supplier Procurement & Landed Costing Procurement Side

4.1 Supplier RFQ & Quotation Comparison /vendor-rate-enquiry, /vendor-quotation-add
Issue RFQs directly tied to customer tender requirements. Log supplier price bids and compare multi-currency rates side-by-side.
4.2 Supplier Purchase Orders (PO) & GRN Inward /vendor-po-add, /vendor-pur-inward-add
Issue formal POs with delivery terms. Goods Receipt Notes (GRN) verify physically received materials at the warehouse against PO line items.
4.3 Complete Multi-Element Landed Cost Framework /vendor-purchase-bill-add, /customs-bill-list
Aggregates 4 distinct cost streams directly into the tender ledger:
Total Project Cost = Vendor Bills + Local Consumables + Freight / Shipping + Customs Duty

MOD-05: Accounts, Treasury & General Ledger Financials

5.1 Cash & Bank Inward / Outward Books /inward-list, /outward-list, /print-voucher/(:num)
Record non-sales income receipts and operational expenditure disbursements (rent, utilities, salaries) with auto-generated receipt and payment vouchers.
5.2 Contra Entries & Petty Cash Float /contra-entry, /petty-cash
Internal fund movements (Cash to Bank, Bank to Bank) without equity impact. Petty cash float management with minor expense vouchers and running balance tracking.
5.3 Credit & Debit Notes /credit-debit-note-list, /credit-debit-note-add
Issue Credit Notes to clients for goods returns or invoice revisions; issue Debit Notes to suppliers for rejected materials or price disputes.

MOD-06: Payment Processing & Settlements Settlements

6.1 Customer Invoice Receipts /customer-invoice-receipt
Real-time outstanding balance calculation. Multi-invoice allocation of incoming cheques, wires, or cash payments with official receipt voucher printouts.
6.2 Vendor Payments & Advance Settlements /vendor-payment-list, /vendor-adv-payment
Disburse advance deposits against Supplier POs or settle outstanding supplier purchase bills with debit reconciliation.

MOD-07: Audit & Financial Statements Audit & Accounting

7.1 Journal Vouchers & Double-Entry Ledger /vouchers-list, /voucher-entries-list
Manual journal vouchers for adjustments and provisions with strict Total Debit == Total Credit validation checks.
7.2 Trial Balance & Profit & Loss (P&L) /trial-balance-list, /profit-loss-report
Consolidated Trial Balance across Assets, Liabilities, Incomes, and Expenses. Automatic P&L report computing gross margin and net operating profit.

MOD-08: Business Intelligence & Tax Reports Reporting Suite

8.1 Tender Enquiry Summary & Profitability Matrix /tender-enquiry-summary-report
Project Profitability Engine: Real-time comparison of Total Invoiced Sales vs. Complete Landed Costs (Supplier POs + Local Bills + Freight + Customs) showing net gross profit and margin %.
8.2 Customer & Vendor Statements of Account (SOA) /customer-statement-report, /vendor-statement-report
Complete chronological ledger statements with opening balances, debits, credits, and closing dues. Supports PDF printing and Excel (.xls) exports.
8.3 NBR & VAT Compliance Reporting /sales-nbr-report, /purchase-nbr-report
Sales VAT output register and Purchase input tax credit (ITC) schedule aligned with NBR and GCC VAT filing standards.
