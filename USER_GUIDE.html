1. End-to-End System Workflow Lifecycle Overview

The ERP-PSM system manages two synchronized operational tracks: Customer Sales & Project Execution and Supplier Procurement & Landed Cost Tracking.

A. Customer Tender-to-Cash Cycle

Step 01
Tender Enquiry
Log customer RFQ, upload specs & register required items.

Step 02
Tender Quotation

Step 03

Record client award, attach PO file & confirm line items.

Step 04
Delivery Challan
Material dispatch, transport info & customer receipt note.

Sales Invoice & Receipt
Generate commercial invoice & record payment receipts.

3. Tender & Sales Execution Guide Customer Side

Operational Step System Route Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Output / Action
1. Tender Enquiry add-tender-enquiry Select Company & Customer → Enter Tender Name & Closing Date → Upload specification PDF → Add required item line items. Enquiry No Generated
2. Tender Quotation tender-quotation-add Select Tender Enquiry → Auto-fetches items → Input Currency, Unit Rates, VAT %, Freight/Handling → Define quotation validity terms. Print Quotation PDF
3. Customer PO customer-tender-po-add Select Customer & Quotation → Input Customer PO Number, Award Date → Upload signed client PO copy & confirm ordered quantities. Order Status: Awarded
4. Delivery Challan (DC) tender-dc-add Select Tender PO → Input Delivery Date, Vehicle No, Driver & Dispatch Quantities → Print Challan for physical gate pass & delivery note. Print DC Note
5. Commercial Invoice tender-invoice-add Select Tender PO & DC → System computes Subtotal, VAT Amount, Net Total → Select remittance bank & verify terms. Print Tax Invoice
6. Payment Collection customer-invoice-receipt Select Customer → Choose Bank / Cash mode → Enter Cheque/Ref No & allocate payment against outstanding invoice numbers. Receipt Voucher

7. Role-Based SOP & Best Practices Operational Standards

Tendering & Sales Team
  • Always attach original PDF specs when creating a Tender Enquiry.
  • Double check foreign currency exchange rates before submitting quotations.
  • Ensure Customer PO copies are uploaded before dispatching materials.
Procurement & Operations Team
  • Always tag Delivery Partner & Customs bills with the corresponding Tender ID.
  • Record Supplier Inward (GRN) immediately upon physical delivery.
  • Obtain customer-stamped Delivery Challans for invoice audit proof.
Accounts & Finance Team
  • Allocate customer receipts against specific invoice numbers for accurate aging.
  • Perform weekly Bank Reconciliation using the Bank Statement tool.
  • Review Tender Summary Report before closing project files for P&L signoff.
System Admin & Management
  • Verify Customer and Vendor Opening Balances at the beginning of each fiscal year.
  • Regularly review the Executive Dashboard KPIs for overdue receivables.
  • Export monthly NBR VAT Return summaries for regulatory tax filing.
